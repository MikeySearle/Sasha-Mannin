Hand-thrown stoneware & porcelain

Pots made to be used every day.

Wheel-thrown and hand-finished in the studio. Every piece one of one.

Clay, water, a wheel and a long wait by the kiln.

Each piece is thrown by hand, trimmed on the wheel, and glazed in small batches — which is why no two ever match exactly. The variation is the point.

Work is made in short runs and sold as it comes out of the kiln. Commissions for tableware, wedding gifts and restaurant service are welcome.

The potter

Sasha Mannin

Sasha has been throwing for over a decade, working mainly in stoneware and porcelain. Her pots are built for the table — plates that stack, cups that sit right in the hand, bowls heavy enough to stay put.

She works in small batches, glazing by hand and firing in a single kiln, which keeps the runs short and the pieces individual.

Alongside the studio work she teaches, and takes on commissions for restaurants, weddings and private collections.

“A pot isn’t finished until someone is using it.”
